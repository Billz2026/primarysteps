Grown-ups: how progress works and older records

Stars are recorded automatically after a verified completion or a correct answer. Repeating the same letter, sound or number is useful practice, but does not earn another My Stars reward. Each activity earns up to three stars at 1, 5 and 12 different completions. There are 15 stars available across the five activities shown.

Active days are counted only after a new distinct completion. The day streak counts consecutive active dates up to today or yesterday. All progress is stored in this browser on this device; it does not sync between devices or user accounts.

Earlier records have not been deleted. Previously verified number tracings are included in completed challenges, but old unverified practice and manually assigned stars are kept separate.
