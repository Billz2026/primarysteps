Grown-ups: sound practice and levels

Listen for the first sound in a spoken word, then pick its lowercase letter. This uses your device's spoken words; it does not reproduce separately recorded pure phonemes. For precise sound modelling, use your school's phonics guidance. Q (usually “qu”) and X (often a final sound) are intentionally not part of this beginning-sound game.

Six successful matches move the automatic mode to the next step. Skipping is always allowed, with no timers. A picture can be shown when listening is difficult. Progress stays on this device and is not linked to My Stars yet.
